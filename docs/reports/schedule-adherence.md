# Schedule Adherence

Welcome to the Help file for the **Schedule Adherence report**! This report displays data on the punctuality of transportation services. Here's a quick rundown on how to read and use this report.

Filter Grouping:
You can filter the data displayed in the report based on whether the services were On-time (within the expected time), early or late. On Time, early, and late grouping are the minutes used for groupings. They are specific to the client's report.

The Adherence Report columns include:
- Date
- Route: The Route name.
- Segment block or Route block: The Segment block name or Route block name.
- Vehicle: The Vehicle name.
- Stop: The Stop name.
- Pax load: The number of on-board passengers when the vehicle exits the stop geofence.
- Arrival: The actual time the vehicle arrives in the stop geofence.
- Departure: The actual time the vehicle departs the stop geofence.
- Time Point: The time the vehicle was scheduled to depart the stop.
- Adherence: The difference between the actual Departure time and the scheduled Time Point. (HH:MM:SS)
  - A '+' adherence time indicates the amount of time vehicle left after the scheduled Time Point.
  - A '-' adherence time indicates the amount of time vehicle left before the scheduled Time Point.
- On Time: The preset interval that the Adherence falls into, as defined by the System Administrator.
