# Service

The **In/Out of Service Report** shows, by time period, each vehicles' period of time In Service or Out Of Service. Filters can be for one or more vehicles, routes, or drivers.
Data is presented as a bar graph - red means out of service and green means in service. By clicking on the line for the vehicle the details of the service period are displayed in a pop up screen. 

**In Service (Definition)**
* Driver selects route and taps In Service on the MDT or....
* Route is selected and vehicle put in service using Dispatch and Messaging in Passio Navigator
* The vehicle is visible on Passio GO and is actively counting passengers. 

**Out of Service (Definition)**
* Driver uses MDT or user logs into Passio Navigator Dispatch & Messaging section to put a vehicle out of service.
* Vehicle DOES NOT show on Passio GO, and no passenger counts will be recorded.

**Report Layout:**
The display of the In Service and Out of Service route details includes:
1. Service Status:
    - The vehicle goes In Service when a driver places the vehicle In Service on the MDT, or a Dispatcher places a vehicle In Service using Navigator Dispatch & Messaging.
    - The vehicle goes Out of Service when a driver places the vehicle Out of Service on the MDT, or a Dispatcher places a vehicle Out of Service using Navigator Dispatch & Messaging.
2. Duration: Time length a vehicle is In Service and/or Out of Service.
3. Driver of vehicle.
4. Route assignment of vehicle.


**Exporting the Report:**
You can export the In/Out of Service Report in CSV, Excel or PDF format. To do this, click on the Export button and select the desired format.
