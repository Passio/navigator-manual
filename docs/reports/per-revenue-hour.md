# Per Revenue Hour

**Report: Ridership per Revenue Distance and Hours**

**Overview:**

This report shows the Ridership comparisons for two different time periods. The report is categorized by days of the week or segment name and consists of columns such as Boardings, Hours, Revenue distance, Boardings per Hour, Boardings per Revenue Distance, and the percentage changes in all these parameters.

**Compare:**

Use this feature to compare the ridership per revenue details of any the two periods of time. The comparison will help you understand the changes in metrics over the two time periods.

**Filter:**

You can filter the report by Segment. For example, if you want to view only the details of Blue Route, select the filter option and choose Blue Route from the list.

**Group By:**

You can group the data by Segment, by Job, by Trip Group or by day in the report. 


**Columns**

Original & Comparision data set:
- Days - the number of days of the week.
- Boardings - This column shows the total number of boardings.
- Hours - This column displays the total operating hours.
- Revenue distance - This column shows the total revenue distance covered.
- Boardings per hour - This column displays the average number of boardings per operating hour.
- Boardings per revenue distance unit - This column shows the average number of boardings per revenue distance unit (mile or kilometer) covered.

The Comparision columns give the percentage change month over month or time period over time period:
- Days change - This column shows the percentage change in the days of the week between the two time periods.
- Hours change: This column displays the percentage change in operating hours between the two time periods.
- Boardings change: This column shows the percentage change in the number of boardings between the two time periods.
- Revenue distance change: This column displays the percentage change in revenue distance covered between the two time periods.
- Boardings per hour change: This column shows the percentage change in the average number of boardings per operating hour between the two time periods.
- Boardings per revenue distance unit change: This column shows the percentage change in the average number of boardings per revenue distance unit (mile or kilometer covered between the two time periods.
