# Solutions & Dates

Records of all Solutions purchased, date activated, and warranty status.
## **DATES DESCRIPTION**
### Initial Date ###
Date of original contract signature by customer or the date the add on quote/addendum is signed by the customer
### Start Date ###
The date that the recurring billing for the added solutions begins
### End Date ###
The last date that the recurring services are billed. If they switch to a new solution or if they cancel completely that would be the date entered.
