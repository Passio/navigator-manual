# Driver Timesheets

Check-In/Out Timesheet User Help File:

The Check-In/Out Timesheet allows you to keep track of employee attendance and HR management. Here's how to use the various fields in the timesheet:

- Driver: This field displays the name of employees who are drivers.
- Email: This field displays the email of employees who have entered their email addresses.
- Route block: This field is for tracking the route blocks of the employees.
- Time: This field helps you to keep record of the time when an employee checks-in and checks-out.
- Approved: This field helps you to keep track if the time sheet has been approved by a supervisor.
- Report: This field allows you to generate a report of the timesheet for analysis.

To check-in an employee, simply enter the employee name, select the block route, and enter the time they checked-in. Similarly, enter the check-out time in the same row and hit enter.

Once you have entered all the necessary data, you can generate the report. To do this, click on the Report field and select the date range for which you wish to generate the report. The report will display attendance details for all the employees during the specified date range.

If the timesheet requires approval from a supervisor, the Approved field will be marked as Yes once the supervisor approves the timesheet.

If you have any further questions or concerns about using the Check-In/Out Timesheet, please contact your HR department for further assistance.
