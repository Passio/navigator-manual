# Incident Log

The Incident log records a number of incidents from both system generated incidents and customer specific incidents. Customer specific incidents may include items that are selectable on the MDT or setup in Navigator to report.

**The report contains the following columns:**
- Date and Time
- Device Name
- Vehicle Name
- Driver Name
- Route short name and Route
- Stop
- Incident
- Incident Reason
- Pax Load: Passengers on board the vehicle
- Out of Service: Service Status
- Photo
- Audio

**Examples of Incidents:**
1. The 'Assigned' incident reports when the Vehicle has been assigned to the Route block or Block through Dispatch or through the MDT.
2. The 'In Service' and 'Out of Service' incidents report when the Vehicle goes In Service or Out of Service either from the MDT or through Dispatch.
3. The 'MDT mute pressed' and MDT unmute pressed' incidents report when the driver presses and holds the 'AVA' button on the MDT in order to Mute or Unmute the AVA system.
4. The 'MDT Power On' and MDT Power Off' incidents report when the MDT boots up or shuts down.
